Members Offers

We value our pensioner and disabled members and want to ensure that they have access to our facilities and services. We offer special discounts and packages to make it easier for them to stay active and healthy.

Pensioners and disabled members get 50% off each deal they choose.

Our team is dedicated to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members, regardless of age or ability. We believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to lead a healthy lifestyle, and we are committed to supporting our pensioner and disabled members in achieving their fitness goals.
